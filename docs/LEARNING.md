# Learning workflow and limits

The primary learning loop is retrieval practice: review due words, introduce a manageable number of new words, recall them without seeing the answer, inspect feedback and use the language in a dialogue, story or writing exercise. Coins are optional decoration; ordinary learning does not require a social pass or property purchase.

## Session planning

A sitting takes at most 10–60 due reviews, scaled to the selected daily study time. A 60-minute plan takes at most 30 due reviews and 10 new words in one sitting. The app pauses new words in that sitting if the review backlog exceeds twice its review limit. Remaining reviews stay saved and can be practised in another sitting; they are not discarded. These are card-count bounds, not enforced timers.

An incorrect recall or missing/wrong article is always scheduled as `again`, even if a keyboard shortcut requests `easy`. A one-character typo classified as `close` can be graded at most `hard`. Correct answers retain the learner's ordinary self-rating choices. Failed cards return later in the same sitting, with up to three extra attempts; they remain due afterward if still unlearned.

The daily new-word setting remains a daily allowance. A smaller sitting may introduce only part of that allowance. The calendar's A1–B2 plan and vocabulary counts are planning aids, not a CEFR test, certificate or guarantee of reaching B2 in 26 weeks. Counts labelled “practised” must not be described as verified mastery. “Mature” is a spaced-repetition interval indicator, not proof of proficiency.

## Content and practice

- Main flashcards contain language-specific vocabulary, articles and available examples.
- Each language has 40 reading stories, with 10 at each of A1, A2, B1 and B2; each includes comprehension questions and explanations.
- Games use a curated 36-word pool. They do not cover the entire main vocabulary catalogue.
- Audio uses browser speech facilities, where supported. Repeating a sentence does not constitute verified pronunciation assessment.
- External AI role-play and writing prompts can be copied into the learner's own ChatGPT or Claude account. Embedded AI is not connected by default, and external conversations/corrections do not automatically sync back.
- Reward goals count eligible distinct learning activities. Completing a reward goal does not necessarily complete every block in the daily study plan.

## Economy

Children retain a gentler, separate parent-managed shop with learning activities and accessories, without social access, cars or property. Main accounts use the 16+ world: every five first-success card reviews earn one coin; first correct game activities earn one; a new story with at least two correct answers earns three. All adult awards, including milestones, share a 20-coin calendar-day cap. Excess awards do not carry forward. Higher outfit, vehicle and property tiers double in price. Existing balances and purchases are preserved.

The virtual world has no cash value, transfers, passive income, rent, debt, paid coin purchases, chance-based prizes or calls. Friends need mutual acceptance and an earned pass. Clubs require earned membership; private clubs use invitations. Messaging itself earns no coins. Ages 16–17 and 18+ have separate social spaces, with blocking, reporting and operator moderation.

## Further learning evaluation

Automated tests verify scheduling, answer-checking, content structure and preservation of progress. They do not measure learner outcomes, comprehensibility or pedagogical effectiveness. Human language review, learner testing and assessment against external CEFR tasks remain necessary before making stronger claims.
