Rewards, the 16+ world, friends and clubs

Children’s rewards are held in parent-managed child profiles. Children cannot access the social world. Main-account rewards include coins, stars, avatar choices, inventory and bounded learning activity records. Fictional coins have no cash value and cannot be traded or transferred. All main-account coin awards share a 20-coin calendar-day cap. Existing balances and purchases remain available.

The optional world is for ages 16 and over. A self-declared age band (16–17 or 18+) is saved in your reward wallet; no date of birth or identity document is collected. This does not verify your age. The bands have separate friend and club spaces, and you cannot switch bands yourself. Contact the operator to correct a mistake or request an age-band update. Ordinary lessons remain available without joining social features.

On joining social features, a pseudonymous profile with a generated alias, invitation code, age band and participation setting is saved in this site’s social database. Account names and email addresses are not displayed. There is no searchable people directory. Friend requests require invitation codes and acceptance. Private clubs require an invitation and your own action to join; codes expire after seven days and can be replaced. Public clubs are visible only within the same age band. Social passes and club memberships are purchased using earned virtual coins.

Messages contain plain text only and are visible to conversation participants or club members. Messages are excluded after 30 days and removed on the next active social request. Reports are retained for up to 90 days and similarly removed on a later active social request. Moderators can review reported messages, hide content and suspend social access. The operator does not provide end-to-end encryption. Do not post sensitive information, contact details or external links. Attachments, calls, recordings, read receipts and presence tracking are not provided.

Blocking hides contact and messages in both directions; reporting hides the reported message for you and sends it to the operator’s moderation inbox. You can leave clubs, remove friends, replace invitations, unblock members or pause social access without losing learning progress. Contact the operator listed in the Impressum to appeal a suspension or request export/deletion of your social records. Database backups, where enabled by the hosting provider, follow the provider’s retention schedule.
